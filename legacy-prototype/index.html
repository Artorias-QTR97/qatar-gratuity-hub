Qatar End of Service Gratuity Rules (Article 54 Guide)

1. Minimum 1 Year Requirement

Under Article 54 of Qatar Labour Law No. 14 of 2004, an employee must complete at least one full year (365 days) of continuous employment to qualify for End of Service Gratuity.

2. How the 3-Week Rule Is Calculated

The statutory minimum gratuity is at least three weeks (21 days) of your last Basic Salary for each year of employment, plus pro-rata credit for partial months and days worked.

3. Notice Period Rules (Article 49)

For service up to 2 years, the statutory notice period is 1 month. If you have completed more than 2 years of service, the statutory notice period is 2 months.
