## 2. QATAR LABOUR LAW MATHEMATICAL ENGINE (EXACT FORMULAS)
Implement a dedicated, unit-tested TypeScript calculation module (`src/utils/qatarLabourMath.ts`) adhering strictly to **Qatar Labour Law No. 14 of 2004**, **Decree-Law No. 18 of 2020**, and **Law No. 9 of 2026**:

### Module A: End-of-Service Gratuity & Complete Final Settlement Calculator
1. **Inputs:**
   - `startDate` (Date) & `endDate` (Date)
   - `unpaidLeaveDays` (Number, default `0` — deducted from total service tenure)
   - `basicSalary` (Number in QAR — last monthly basic wage, excluding allowances)
   - `grossSalary` (Number in QAR — full monthly remuneration including housing, transport, and fixed allowances)
   - `contractGratuityDaysPerYear` (Number, default `21` statutory minimum; allow custom input $\ge 21$ for enhanced contracts)
   - `unusedLeaveDays` (Number, default `0`)
   - `leaveCalculationBase` (Toggle: `30` calendar days statutory standard vs. `26` working days contractual option)
   - `unpaidSalaryDays` (Number of days worked in the final month not yet paid)
   - `noticeDaysOwedToEmployee` (Number of unserved notice period days if employer terminates without full notice)
   - `repatriationTicketOwed` (Number in QAR, optional one-way home ticket value under Art. 57)
   - `deductions` (Number in QAR, documented company loans/advances under Art. 54/70)

2. **Exact Legal Logic & Formulas:**
   - **Net Service Days:**
     $$\text{Net Days} = \max(0, (\text{endDate} - \text{startDate in days}) + 1 - \text{unpaidLeaveDays})$$
   - **Service Years (Decimal):**
     $$\text{Service Years} = \frac{\text{Net Days}}{365.25}$$
   - **Eligibility Check (Article 54):**
     - If $\text{Net Days} < 365$: `isEligibleForGratuity = false`, `gratuityAmount = 0` (Display tooltip explaining Article 54 requires 1 full year of continuous service, and note that resignation vs. termination does NOT reduce gratuity in Qatar unless dismissed under Article 61).
     - If $\text{Net Days} \ge 365$: `isEligibleForGratuity = true`.
   - **Gratuity Calculation (Articles 54 & 72 — Computed strictly on BASIC Salary):**
     Provide a precision toggle with two modes:
     - *Standard Qatar HR Practice (Default):*
       $$\text{Gratuity} = \left(\frac{\text{basicSalary}}{30}\right) \times \text{contractGratuityDaysPerYear} \times \left(\frac{\text{Net Days}}{365}\right)$$
     - *Strict 3-Week Statutory Floor:*
       $$\text{Gratuity} = \left(\text{basicSalary} \times \frac{12}{52}\right) \times 3 \times \left(\frac{\text{Net Days}}{365}\right)$$
   - **Unused Annual Leave Encashment (Articles 72, 79 & 81 — Computed on BASIC Salary):**
     - Display statutory entitlement helper badge: `< 5 years service = 21 days/year (3 weeks)`; `>= 5 years service = 28 days/year (4 weeks)`.
     - Formula:
       $$\text{Leave Encashment} = \left(\frac{\text{basicSalary}}{\text{leaveCalculationBase}}\right) \times \text{unusedLeaveDays}$$
   - **Notice Period Pay in Lieu (Article 49 as amended by Decree-Law No. 18 of 2020 — Computed on FULL GROSS REMUNERATION):**
     - Auto-detect statutory notice requirement:
       - If $\text{Service Years} \le 2.0$: Statutory notice = **1 Month (30 days)**.
       - If $\text{Service Years} > 2.0$: Statutory notice = **2 Months (60 days)**.
     - CRITICAL LEGAL DISTINCTION: Highlight in the UI that unlike Gratuity (Art. 54) and Leave Pay (Art. 72), **Article 49 Notice Pay is legally calculated on Full Gross Salary (`grossSalary`)**, not Basic Salary:
       $$\text{Notice Pay} = \left(\frac{\text{grossSalary}}{30}\right) \times \text{noticeDaysOwedToEmployee}$$
   - **Unpaid Worked Days in Final Month (Computed on Gross Salary):**
     $$\text{Final Month Wage} = \left(\frac{\text{grossSalary}}{30}\right) \times \text{unpaidSalaryDays}$$
   - **Total Net Final Settlement:**
     $$\text{Total Settlement} = \text{Gratuity} + \text{Leave Encashment} + \text{Notice Pay} + \text{Final Month Wage} + \text{repatriationTicketOwed} - \text{deductions}$$
   - **Statutory Payment Deadline Alert (Article 67 & Law No. 9 of 2026):**
     Display an automatic compliance banner on the result card stating: *"Under Article 67 of the Qatar Labour Law, all final settlement dues must be paid before the end of the next working day after your contract ends (or within 7 days if you left without notice)."*

## 3. SECURITY, PRIVACY & QATAR COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS

### A. Qatar PDPPL (Law No. 13 of 2016) "Zero-Data" Architecture
1. **Strict Zero-Server Storage:** The application must NEVER transmit salary numbers, employment dates, or user inputs to any backend server, analytics payload, or third-party database. All state must reside strictly in volatile React memory (cleared on tab close).
2. **No PII Collection:** Do NOT create input fields for Qatar ID (QID), Passport Number, Phone Number, Email Address, or Bank IBAN.
3. **Optional Print Labels Only:** On the "Download/Print Official PDF Statement" modal, allow two optional local-only text inputs (`Employee Name / Reference` and `Employer Name`) solely for rendering on the printed page, accompanied by a prominent lock badge: *"100% Private: Processed locally in your browser. Never sent to or stored on any server."*
4. **Input Sanitization (XSS Prevention):** Strictly sanitize and escape all user inputs before rendering them into the printable DOM/PDF view. Restrict numeric inputs to finite bounds (`0` to `10,000,000` QAR; dates between `1970-01-01` and `2050-12-31`).
